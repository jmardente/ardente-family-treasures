Meet the Author

Hi, I'm Jamie.

I've always loved the way a good story can make us laugh, feel, wonder, and dream. Today, I bring that love of storytelling to children's adventures, emotional fiction, romance, and creative projects.

As JM Ardente, I create imaginative ocean adventures for young readers. As Jamie Ardente, I explore the complicated, beautiful emotions that make us human.

I also enjoy helping others shape their ideas into stories and visual worlds, combining a writer's imagination with modern creative tools.

Jamie
