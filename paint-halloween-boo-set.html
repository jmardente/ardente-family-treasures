Halloween Boo Set

Easy, fun and beginner friendly. Your piece does not have to look exactly like ours — the best part is making it yours.

Paint colors: White, black, pumpkin orange, purple, green, pink for cheeks, gray for optional shading, and yellow for the pumpkin. Acrylic craft paint works well.
1. Get ready
Make sure each ceramic piece is clean and dry. Wipe away any dust before painting.
2. Paint the base colors
Paint the ghost white. On the BOO piece, paint the letters orange, purple and black. Use thin coats and let each coat dry before adding another.
3. Add the faces
Use a small brush for the ghost eyes and mouth. Add pink cheeks if you like. Paint the little ghosts on the BOO piece white and add their faces after the white paint dries.
4. Add the fun details
Paint the bat, bow, cat, swirls, dots, pumpkin and witch hat. Don't worry about making every line perfect — hand-painted is supposed to look handmade.
5. Finishing touches
Add optional light gray shading or highlights for dimension. Once everything is completely dry, you can protect your project with a clear acrylic sealer in matte or glossy finish.
Tips for success: Use several thin coats instead of one thick coat. Let colors dry before painting nearby details. A small detail brush makes faces and dots much easier. Most importantly, have fun with it.
